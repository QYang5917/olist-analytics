# Results Summary: the 5 most important findings

Window: Jan 2017 to Aug 2018 · 97,905 orders · 94,703 customers · R$15.7M revenue

1. **Late delivery is the biggest driver of bad reviews.**
   - Late orders average **2.27 stars vs 4.29** on time (−2.02; 95% CI −2.06 to −1.98).
   - 62% of late orders get 1–2 stars, vs 9% of on-time orders.
   - Only 6.7% of orders are late, yet they produce **32.6% of all 1–2 star reviews**.
   - Even 1–3 days late drops the average to 3.29. The gap holds within all 21 states tested.

2. **Customers punish broken promises, not slow shipping.**
   - Across states, late rate correlates **−0.89** with review score; raw delivery days correlate only **−0.46**.
   - AM and AP take 26–27 days but have honest estimates (about 3% late) and review well (4.24).
   - AL and MA miss their estimates 18–22% of the time and have the worst reviews (3.83–3.85).
   - **Action:** fix delivery estimates in the Northeast first; it's cheaper than faster logistics.

3. **Almost nobody comes back.**
   - Only **2.0% of customers buy again within 180 days** (2.2% ever).
   - Month-1 cohort retention is about **0.5%**, and it's flat across all cohorts.
   - Growth has been entirely new-customer acquisition, so retention is the biggest untapped lever.

4. **Growth more than doubled, then stalled.**
   - Revenue for Jan–Aug 2018 was **R$8.59M, +140% year over year**.
   - But monthly revenue stayed flat at R$0.98–1.16M from January to August 2018.
   - Black Friday 2017 is still the peak month (R$1.17M). Average order value is steady at about R$160, so growth = more orders.

5. **Category winners and losers only show up when measured against the market.**
   - The platform grew 138%, so raw growth makes nearly every category look like a winner.
   - **Gaining share:** watches_gifts (+2.95 pp) and health_beauty (+2.46 pp), which are now #1 and #2 by revenue.
   - **Losing share:** cool_stuff (−3.74 pp; it grew only 9%), garden_tools (−1.74 pp), toys (−1.57 pp).

**Bonus: A/B test. Don't launch the new landing page.**
- New page 11.87% vs old page 12.03% conversion (−0.16 pp, p = 0.20, 288,586 users).
- 95% CI for the difference: −0.39 to +0.08 pp, so the best plausible upside is about +0.7%, not worth a rollout.
- Clean randomization (SRM p = 0.92), no novelty effect, and the same result under both cleaning rules.
